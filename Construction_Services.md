# LancersTech Construction ERP: Services Overview

## Empowering Construction Management through Integrated Technology

Our ERP solution is designed to handle the complex, multi-site operational needs of modern construction companies. By integrating procurement, workforce management, and financial control into a single platform, we help you reduce waste, optimize labor costs, and ensure project timelines are met.

---

### 1. Smart Material & Inventory Management
*Precision control over your most valuable assets.*
- **Real-time Stock Tracking**: Monitor levels of critical materials like cement, steel, bricks, and electrical components across multiple sites.
- **Automated Low-Stock Alerts**: Receive proactive notifications before materials run out to prevent project delays.
- **Procurement & Supplier Management**: Manage your vendor database and generate purchase orders directly within the system.
- **Stock Logs**: Maintain a transparent audit trail of every material movement, reducing shrinkage and unauthorized usage.

### 2. Digital Workforce & Site Attendance
*Connecting the office to the field.*
- **Geofenced/QR-based Attendance**: (Adaptable) Ensure workers are on-site before they can clock in.
- **Shift & Department Management**: Organize engineers, site supervisors, and manual laborers into structured teams.
- **Flexible Pay Structures**: Handle both permanent staff (monthly salary) and daily-wage/contractual workers (hourly rates).
- **Leave Management**: Digitalize leave requests and approvals to maintain optimal staffing levels on-site.

### 3. Project Finance & Expense Control
*Total transparency on project profitability.*
- **Granular Expense Tracking**: Categorize every rupee spent—from machinery maintenance to fuel and site miscellaneous costs.
- **Automated Payroll**: Generate professional payslips and manage payroll runs with a single click, accounting for attendance and deductions.
- **Cost Analysis**: Compare budgeted vs. actual expenses to identify cost overruns in real-time.

### 4. Tendering & Bid Management
*Winning more projects with data.*
- **Bid Tracking**: Centralize all your project tenders and active bids.
- **Quotation Management**: Streamline the creation and submission of technical and financial proposals.

### 5. Centralized Data & Analytics
*Data-driven decision making.*
- **Dashboard Overview**: A bird's-eye view of your company's health—total sales, employee count, and stock status.
- **Secure Access Control**: Role-based permissions ensure that site supervisors, HR managers, and senior executives only see the data relevant to them.
- **Audit Trails**: Every action is logged, ensuring accountability across your entire organization.
